不用困難單字，從簡短、破碎的話，長成完整的想法

一個想法
I usually try to get something different for lunch.

一個完整的表達
Unlike those who tend to have the same thing for lunch every day, I usually try to get something different, which is why I find food delivery apps really convenient.

B: Join the club. I've already had three cups of boba this week, so your secret is safe with me.

一、辦公室茶水間和同事閒聊
1.比較兩句的差異：
(1) I want to eat another piece of cake, but I'm currently on a diet.
直白，無畫面，難吸引人
(2) My desire to eat another piece of cake seems to outweigh my commitment to my summer diet.
精準描繪出「吃蛋糕的慾望與和減肥承諾的抗衡」之心理狀態
2.如何說句子出(2)：
了解英文片語和子句當形容詞和中文形容詞在位置的差異：名詞＋形容詞語塊【後位修飾】
desire + to eat ... cake
學習者更容易理解與實踐第三人稱單數主詞desire搭配單數動詞seems

二、展覽館場前排隊攀談
A: My desire to see this exhibition seems to outweigh my hatred of waiting in long lines.
B: Same here. My legs are already dead, but I'm in too deep to leave now.
A: 我想要看這個展覽的渴望，似乎已經勝過了我對排長隊的討厭。
B: 我也是。我的腿已經快廢了，但現在放棄也太可惜了。
