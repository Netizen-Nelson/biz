所謂「說出完整的想法」，第一步不是學更多單字，而是學會讓一個核心詞承載更多資訊。

不靠夢幻單字，也能從簡短、破碎的話，長成完整的想法

一個想法
I usually try to get something different for lunch.

一個完整的表達
Unlike those who tend to have the same thing for lunch every day, I usually try to get something different, which is why I find food delivery apps really convenient.

(1) I want to eat another piece of cake, but I'm currently on a diet.

Lesson 1-6 Do you have any weekend plans?


No, I don't have any plans. Maybe I'll just stay home and watch Netflix.

缺點
話題終結： 直接回答「沒有計畫」，容易讓氣氛陷入尷尬
被動對答： 未提供細節或話題切入點，對方很難順著接話
缺乏互動： 句末沒有反問，少了繼續交流的機會


Not really! I just have a long list of shows to binge-watch on Netflix. I've been working so hard lately, so my main goal is just to find some time to unwind. How about you? Do you have any fun places to go?

社交策略：答題、拓展、鉤子
提供細節： 用binge-watch, unwind讓對話生動有畫面感
引發共鳴： 提及「最近工作辛苦」，以共通經驗引出共同話題
主動發球： 反問How about you?把對話主導權還給對方

替換 to hang out with friends to chill on a sunny weekend to take photos for Instagram to grab a late-night bite to enjoy your time in Sydney

閱讀這篇短文，感受畫面與情緒 In Taiwan, taking out the trash is a collective ritual. We don't just leave it on the curb; we have to wait for the yellow truck that plays Beethoven's Für Elise. Once that music starts, you'll see residents in slippers rushing down, holding their bags, big and small. It's not only a daily routine when the whole neighborhood comes alive, but also a race against the clock because the truck won't wait. 在台灣，倒垃圾是一種集體儀式。 我們不只是把垃圾袋丟在路邊；我們必須等那輛播著貝多芬《給愛麗絲》的黃色垃圾車。 只要那段音樂一響起，就會看到左鄰右舍穿著拖鞋衝下樓，大包小包全握在手裡。 這不只是整個社區每天共同甦醒的日常，更是一場與時間的賽跑，因為垃圾車不等人。 分析語塊和語序，說出相應的英文
  1. 外國人很難理解我們每天與垃圾車賽跑的難處。
  2. 在音樂響起前搶先下樓的時機，只是幾秒鐘的事。
  3. 那個手提垃圾袋衝下樓的決定，是幾千個台灣人每天傍晚都會做的事。
  4. 提早準備好垃圾袋的計畫，讓我變成整棟樓最從容的那個人。
  1. People from abroad hardly ever understand our daily struggle to race against the trash truck.
  2. The window to beat your neighbors downstairs before the music starts is only a matter of seconds.
  3. The decision to rush downstairs, bags in hand, is one that thousands of Taiwanese make every single evening.
  4. My plan to have the bags ready ahead of time makes me the most composed person in the whole building.
